## Answer

LLM‑based critics detect hallucinated claims by treating factuality assessment as a supervised verification task that leverages both internal consistency checks and external evidence.  ChainPoll, for example, frames detection as a chain‑of‑thought prompting problem and achieves the highest AUROC (0.781) across RealHall benchmarks, outperforming other theoretical methods by 11% and exceeding industry standards by over 23% (Robert and Sanyal, 2023)<sup>C20, C21</sup>.  Similarly, OpenFactCheck incorporates a dedicated LLMEVAL module that evaluates LLM outputs from multiple factuality perspectives, providing a systematic framework for fact‑checking (Wang et al., 2024)<sup>C27</sup>.

Self‑verification and orchestration strategies further improve detection by having the model critique its own answers.  Ahtisham et al. show that self‑verification nearly doubles agreement with human annotators compared to unverified baselines, and orchestration boosts Cohen’s kappa for qualitative coding by 58% (Ahtisham et al., 2026)<sup>C23, C24</sup>.  These approaches illustrate that prompting LLMs to generate verification chains or to cross‑check their statements can markedly raise reliability.

However, not all LLMs serve as effective judges.  Fu et al. report that factuality scores from GPT‑4 and PaLM‑2 do not correlate significantly with human evaluations, while only GPT‑3.5 shows modest correlation across two subcategories (Fu et al., 2023)<sup>C25, C26</sup>.  Moreover, retrieval‑augmented methods improve factuality evaluation but current models still struggle to detect factual errors, especially across diverse domains and geographic regions, where performance gaps persist between statements from the Global North and South (Chen et al., 2023; Coelho et al., 2025)<sup>C30, C31, C32</sup>.

Overall, the evidence suggests that LLM‑based critics rely on chain‑of‑thought prompting, self‑verification, modular design, and retrieval augmentation to spot hallucinations, yet their effectiveness varies by model size, prompting strategy, and domain context, indicating room for further refinement.
